01 / THE PROJECT

MORE THAN
JUST CLOTHING.

Collection 001 is the beginning of an independent streetwear brand built around original designs, individual expression, and a recognizable identity.

The vision is to create clothing that combines dark gothic influences with vintage and grunge aesthetics.

Every release will feature original concepts, carefully selected materials, and designs developed to establish a consistent brand identity.

Rather than producing large quantities of untested inventory, the business will initially use limited pre-order collections to understand customer demand and fund production responsibly.

04 / THE PLAN

FROM IDEA TO REALITY.

The first release is only the beginning. Each stage is designed to build the foundation for a sustainable clothing business.

PHASE 01 — BRAND DEVELOPMENT

Finalize the brand identity, clothing designs, graphics, typography, and product direction.

PHASE 02 — SAMPLING

Contact manufacturers, compare production quotes, order samples, and evaluate quality.

PHASE 03 — FIRST RELEASE

Launch Collection 001 with a limited pre-order period and clearly communicated production terms.

PHASE 04 — FIRST $1,000

Work toward the first $1,000 in gross sales. Fulfill customer orders and evaluate the actual cost and profitability of production.

PHASE 05 — EXPANSION

Reinvest available profits, introduce new designs, improve production, and build future collections.
